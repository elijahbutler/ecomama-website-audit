# Ecomama Farms

## Platform
web

## Product and users
A public field journal for a working biodynamic rose farm and private estate gatherings in Santa Ynez. Visitors include couples, private hosts, floral clients, planners, and their guests.

## Primary tasks
Understand the working farm, explore estate spaces, collect flower and table references, begin an honest inquiry, and find useful valley destinations.

## Accepted direction
The implementation follows DESIGN_AUDIT_AND_PROPOSAL.md and the October 26 implementation brief. Farm work leads the story. Event planning remains easy to find. Preserve the brand typography and actual farm photographs.

## Stack
React 18, TypeScript, React Router 6, Vite 5, Tailwind 3, and Lucide. Native controls, SVG geometry, and CSS cover the new interactions without additional runtime dependencies.

## Content boundaries
The approved brief establishes 2,500+ heirloom rose bushes, 30+ acres, biodynamic growing, Santa Ynez soil, dawn cuts, and private estate gatherings. Cultivar identities, precise bloom windows, rental materials, estate geography, pond completion, and new workshops are not established. Preserve those uncertainties in the interface and data.

## Inquiry contract
Prepare a visitor-controlled mailto draft addressed to contact@ecomamafarms.com. No server submission or receipt claim. The published wedding form is a separate external path. Keep personal data out of shareable preference URLs and storage.

## Accessibility and performance
All controls work with a keyboard and have visible names, focus, and selected states. Honour reduced motion. Target 180 KB compressed initial JavaScript, 250 KB mobile hero, and 450 KB desktop hero. These are budgets, not field performance claims.

## Navigation and content expansion

The full navigation exposes the original estate, farm, floral, explore, and about pages through dropdowns and mobile accordions. Venue and Vignettes each cover six facilities and six portrait locations. Floral explains the heirloom and David Austin roses and includes a larger archive lookbook. Services separates full-service weddings, venue-only buyouts, and custom gatherings, with access windows confirmed in the proposal. Story and Sustainability remain standalone routes.

Contact separates the wedding CRM intake from general inquiries. General inquiries still prepare a visitor-sent email draft, with an explicit routing note and no connected webhook.
